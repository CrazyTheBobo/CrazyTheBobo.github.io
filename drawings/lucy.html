Lucy 11 · Aug · 2023 This followed almost immediately after completing Cyberpunk: Edgerunners, one of the most soul-shattering stories I have encountered. I loved the futility of it all, the despondence, the perfect irony of oversaturated neon colours juxtaposed against the story’s bleak hopelessness. I felt Lucy to be a metaphor for the viewer: catalysing the start, but subsequently becoming a vessel whose emotions mirrored my own. In this expression I sought to capture the mixture of hopelessness and quiet grief that trauma so often imprints on the individual.
