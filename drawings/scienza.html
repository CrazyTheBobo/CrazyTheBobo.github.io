Scienza · Apr · 2021 This marked a milestone in my drawing journey; It was the first oil painting I was truly happy with. The colours are simple to the extent of being dichromic, but the focus was always to hone my proficiency with brushwork and my understanding of focal point, contrast and precision of construction. A year and a half on since the first time I drew this bust in pencil, it represented the closing of one cycle, and the beginning of another. Therein lay its symbolic value.
