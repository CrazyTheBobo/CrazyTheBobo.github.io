My Last War 09 · Mar · 2021 My first serious digital painting, completed in a single session of intense focus spanning 7 hours. Since reading the ending scenes of Attack on Titan, I had been captivated by Eren’s expression in this panel, and all the baggage it silently represented. I saw a man who was broken, relieved, resigned, and above all, weary of the world.
