Longing 20 · Sep · 2021 This was one of the few dream scenes I captured. It was a night cyberpunk city in the midst of a powerful sandstorm tinted with sodium yellow. I remember standing atop a skyscraper that loomed over the city, the other architectures so dwarfed by its enormity that only their lights could be seen. I was wearing a black robe, and in the midst of the storm I stood, heart longing for something just beyond reach.
