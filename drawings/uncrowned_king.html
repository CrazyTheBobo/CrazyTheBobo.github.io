The Uncrowned King 29 · Jun · 2024 I drew this while on the long stretch back to Singapore from London, after changing at Bahrain. I had seen the original picture of Kevin Levrone a few weeks back, and I was captivated by the sheer, monstrous beauty of his symmetry and proportions. It was a return to simple pencil sketching - where my origins lay - and it was refreshing, meditative.
