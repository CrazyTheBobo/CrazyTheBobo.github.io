Do I Look Like a God, or a Devil? 10 · Sep · 2022 The quote comes from Alice in Borderlands, a battle royale-style manga whose philosophical overtones became increasingly obvious towards the conclusion. It extends to the ontological question I believe many of us find ourselves asking at some point in their life - is this world heaven, or hell? If there is a creator, is he a God, or a Devil? Sometimes, angels play disguised with devils’ faces, and other times, the opposite is true.
