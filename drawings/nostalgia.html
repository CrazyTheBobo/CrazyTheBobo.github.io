Nostalgia 10 · Nov · 2021 A drawing of father at the holiday villa in Pantai Cenang, Langkawi. I experimented with rough, free brushstrokes to build up the canvas in shapes rather than lines, as well as a dynamic perspective distortion. I think the colours came together quite well, evoking the tropical sun of a beachside summer.
