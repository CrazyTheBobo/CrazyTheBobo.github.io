Louvre 25 · Apr · 2024 On the final day of a 3-day Paris fling, O-Teen and I found ourselves with an overabundance of time which we filled by busying ourselves with this scene. For me, the Louvre has occupied a special space in my heart as an artist, in particular due to its extensive collection of Leonardos. It was a beautifully sunny day and I sought, as a foremost objective, to capture the colours rather than the form.
